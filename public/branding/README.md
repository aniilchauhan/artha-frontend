# Artha AI App Logo & Brand Identity

Created with the built-in image generation tool. The master artwork is `artha-logo.png` / `axion-logo.png`; launcher and browser sizes are exported from that artwork.

## Concept & Name Origin

- **Name**: **Artha AI** (अर्थ — Wealth, Purpose & Economic Intelligence, rooted in Sanskrit and classical Indian economic treatise *Arthashastra*).
- **Symbol**: **The Neural Synaptic Tree of Compounding Wealth (Kalpavriksha)** — A stylized sacred tree with glowing roots and fiber-optic trunk, expanding into an illuminated canopy of circular alpha coin nodes rendered in signature Google Antigravity luminous mesh gradients (ultraviolet purple, neon magenta, and electric cyber cyan) on a deep midnight void canvas.

## Generation Prompt

> Production-ready square app icon and brand logo for Artha AI, a high-performance stock market financial intelligence terminal. The Neural Synaptic Tree of Compounding Wealth: a stylized minimalist modern sacred tree where the roots and trunk are formed from glowing fiber-optic synaptic lines that branch upward into an illuminated canopy of glowing circular alpha coin nodes. Radiant Google Antigravity style luminous mesh gradients blending deep ultraviolet purple, vivid neon magenta, electric cyber cyan, and celestial blue on a solid dark midnight navy canvas #070913. Perfectly centered within the middle 65% of the canvas with generous margins for squircle and circular app icon cropping. Futuristic fintech identity, ultra-clean vector graphic design, radiant glowing neon accents. Square 1:1 canvas, no text, no letters, no wordmark, no 3D device frames.
